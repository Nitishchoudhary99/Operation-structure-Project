Everything around the e-way bill

₹50,000 warning

A sale, delivery note, purchase or return over ₹50,000 without an e-way bill number shows a warning with a “Make e-way bill” button.

JSON in one tap

The bill becomes the official bulk-upload JSON: GSTINs, addresses, PIN codes, HSN, units, tax, distance, vehicle or transporter ID.

Validity worked out

Save the 12-digit number, date and distance: “valid till” is worked out (1 day per 200 km, 20 km for over-dimensional cargo) and printed on the bill.

Expiry alerts

One screen lists bills over the limit with no e-way bill, and e-way bills running out in 2 days or already expired.

Purchases and returns too

Inward e-way bills for purchase bills (with the supplier’s bill number) and sales returns, outward ones for goods sent back to a supplier.

Ship to / dispatch from

Goods going to a godown or site that is not the billing address, or leaving from another place: bill-to and ship-to are both filled in.

How it works

  1. Make the invoice, delivery note or purchase bill as usual.
  2. Tap E-way bill, enter distance and vehicle number, and download the JSON.
  3. Upload it at ewaybillgst.gov.in → e-Waybill → Generate Bulk.
  4. Save the e-way bill number on the bill: validity and alerts are set by themselves.

Good to know

  • E-way bill and e-invoice JSON are in the Growth and Business plans
  • The e-way bill is generated on the government portal from the JSON; the app does not log in to the portal for you
  • The e-way bill number and validity print on the invoice

Frequently asked questions

Is an e-way bill needed for every bill?

It is needed when goods worth more than ₹50,000 move, for a sale, purchase, return or transfer. Some states set a different limit for movement inside the state. The app warns on every document over ₹50,000 that has no e-way bill number.

Does the app generate the e-way bill number itself?

It prepares the official bulk-upload JSON. You upload it on ewaybillgst.gov.in and save the number back on the bill. Direct generation through a GSP is not offered yet.

How is e-way bill validity worked out?

One day for every 200 km or part of it (20 km for over-dimensional cargo), ending at midnight. Save the date and distance and the app shows “valid till” and warns before it runs out.

Can I make an e-way bill for a purchase or a return?

Yes. Purchase bills and sales returns give an inward e-way bill; goods returned to a supplier give an outward one.

What if the goods go to a different address?

Fill in the ship-to address in the e-way bill box. The bill-to party stays the same and the e-way bill shows where the goods really go.
