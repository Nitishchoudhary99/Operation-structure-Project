Why shops switch to SmartTrader Khata

Entry counts only after it is sent

A new entry is saved only when you send it to the customer, so nobody can say "yeh entry kab likhi?"

Customer confirms each entry

The customer opens a private link, sees every entry and the balance, and taps Yes or No. Disputes show up for you at once.

No silent edits

Sent entries cannot be deleted. A mistake is corrected with a reverse entry that the customer also receives.

WhatsApp & SMS reminders

One tap sends a reminder with the balance and your UPI. The Payment Reminders screen lists everyone who owes you.

Pay by UPI from the link

The customer gets a Pay button, a QR and your UPI ID, so collection is faster.

Khata + billing + stock

Unlike a plain khata app, the same account also does GST bills, stock and full accounts when you need them.

How it works

  1. Tap You gave or You got and enter the amount.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp or SMS. The entry is saved.
  3. The customer opens the link, confirms the entry and pays by UPI.

Payment promises that are kept

  • The customer picks a pay date on their link (“Kab tak denge?”), or pays in instalments
  • The Today card shows whose promise falls due today
  • A monthly balance confirmation the customer answers with one tap

Frequently asked questions

Is the khata free?

Yes. Khata entries, reminders and customer links are free and unlimited on every plan.

Can the customer see their khata?

Yes. Every message carries a private link where the customer sees all entries, the balance, and can confirm or dispute each entry.

Can I delete a wrong entry?

An entry that has not been sent can be discarded. A sent entry stays on record; you reverse it with a correction entry that is also sent to the customer.

Does it send payment reminders?

Yes. Send a reminder with the balance and your UPI ID on WhatsApp or SMS from the contact or from the Payment Reminders screen.

Is it a Khatabook alternative?

Yes. It covers udhaar tracking and reminders, and adds customer confirmation, GST billing, stock and accounts in the same app.
