Moving from Khatabook

  1. Export your items, customers and suppliers from Khatabook as an Excel or CSV file.
  2. In SmartTrader, open Items (then Customers & Suppliers) and tap Import from Excel; Khatabook export columns are recognised, and you check every row before saving.
  3. Add each customer’s opening balance and your stock in hand.
  4. Make your first bill. Keep both apps side by side for a few days if you like; nothing is locked in.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my data from Khatabook?

Yes. Export items, customers and suppliers as Excel or CSV and use Import from Excel on the Items and Customers screens; Khatabook export columns are recognised, and nothing is saved until you check the rows and tap Import.

Is SmartTrader free?

Yes. The Free plan gives 25 GST invoices a month, 1 user, unlimited khata, POS and all reports. Unlimited bills start at Rs 999 a year.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Every screen can be in English or Hindi, and bills and reminders go to customers in the language you choose.

Is my data safe?

Each business is kept separate, staff get role-based access with an audit trail, and you can download a full backup any time.
