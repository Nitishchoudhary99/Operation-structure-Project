Frequently asked questions

Can salesmen use it with limited access?

Yes. Each role decides what a user may see and change, and limits like the biggest discount, no credit sales or no back-dated bills are enforced on the server.

Does it handle sales orders and partial deliveries?

Yes. Orders track what is delivered and billed, and the pending orders report shows what is still open.

Can I generate e-way bills?

It creates the e-way bill JSON for upload on the e-way bill portal and saves the e-way bill number on the invoice (Growth plan and above).

How do collections from field staff work?

Staff record the cash collected against the customer; a receipt goes to the customer on WhatsApp and the owner sees who collected what.

Can I export to Tally?

Yes. Export a Tally XML for any period; the trial balance matches.
