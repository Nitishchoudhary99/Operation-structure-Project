Frequently asked questions

Can I keep track of where items are kept?

Yes. Set a rack / bin per item; pick lists are sorted by rack and new bins can be made as goods come in.

Can I make quotations?

Yes. Quotations convert to sales orders or invoices, and pending quantities are tracked.

Can contractors buy on credit?

Yes. Credit sales go to the customer’s khata, with reminders and balance confirmations.

What if goods arrive broken?

Write the broken pieces off as a loss or a claim on the transporter or insurer, and track the claim until it is paid.
