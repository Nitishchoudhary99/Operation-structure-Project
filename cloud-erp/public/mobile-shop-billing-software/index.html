Frequently asked questions

Can I track IMEI numbers?

Yes. Serial-tracked items take an IMEI or serial on purchase and on sale, and each serial’s history is kept.

How do I find who bought a phone?

Search the IMEI in Serial / IMEI; it shows the bill, the customer, the date and the warranty end.

Can I bill accessories with a barcode?

Yes. Scan with a USB scanner or the phone camera on the Quick Bill screen.

Does a return update the stock?

Yes. Returning a handset puts the same serial back in stock.
