Reminders that work

Smart schedule

Who to remind today: 3 days before the due date, on it, then 7, 15, 30 and 45 days late. VIP customers get gentler words.

Bill link with UPI

Every reminder carries a link to the customer’s bills with a Pay button and QR for the amount due.

In their language

Messages in English, Hindi or Hinglish, chosen per customer.

Pay date from the customer

On the link the customer picks when they will pay, or splits it into instalments; you see it on the Today card.

Balance confirmation

Once a month, customers confirm their balance with one tap; a wrong bill shows up now, not months later.

Weekly report

A short weekly summary: collected, promised and still overdue.

When reminders are not enough

  • A recovery file with every bill, reminder and promise, ready for a notice
  • MSMED Act interest for registered micro and small enterprises
  • Late-payment report by age

How it works

  1. Open Payment Reminders: today’s list is ready.
  2. Tap Send: WhatsApp opens with the message, link and UPI.
  3. The customer pays from the link or picks a date.

Frequently asked questions

Does it send WhatsApp messages from my number?

Yes. Reminders open in your own WhatsApp with the message ready; you tap send.

What does the customer receive?

The amount due, a link to their bills with a UPI Pay button and QR, and your shop name.

Can customers say when they will pay?

Yes. The link has “When will you pay?” where they pick a date or instalments, and you see it in the app.

Is it free?

Reminders and customer links are free on every plan.
