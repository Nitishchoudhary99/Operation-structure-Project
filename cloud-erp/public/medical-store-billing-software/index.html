Batch and expiry, handled for you

Batch on every bill

Each batch keeps its own expiry and stock. Selling picks the batch that expires first (FEFO), and you can choose another.

Expiry alerts

The Today card shows expired stock and what expires in the next 30 days, with its value, so you can return or sell it in time.

Expiry report

All batches by expiry date and godown, with quantities and value, ready to send back to the supplier.

Returns put the batch back

A sales return or a cancelled bill restores the same batch and expiry, so stock stays right.

Supplier bills in seconds

GST by item

Frequently asked questions

Does it track batch and expiry?

Yes. Every batch has its own expiry and stock, and bills, returns and stock reports all work by batch.

Does it sell the earliest expiry first?

Yes. Billing picks the batch that expires first (FEFO) by default; you can pick a different batch on the line.

Will it warn me before medicines expire?

Yes. The Today card and the expiry report list expired stock and stock expiring within 30 days, with its value.

Can I enter supplier bills quickly?

Yes. Take a photo or open the PDF of the supplier bill; it is read on your phone and the purchase bill is filled in for you to check.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. 50 invoices a month free; unlimited bills from Rs 999 a year.
