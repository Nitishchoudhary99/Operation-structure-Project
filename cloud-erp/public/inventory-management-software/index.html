Frequently asked questions

Does it support multiple godowns?

Yes. Stock is kept per godown, with transfers between them and per-godown stock values.

Can I scan barcodes?

Yes. Use a USB/Bluetooth scanner or the phone camera on the Quick Bill screen, and print barcode labels for items.

Is there a GRN and gate entry?

Yes, in Advanced mode: log the vehicle at the gate, then make the GRN from the gate entry against the purchase order.

How is stock valued?

At moving-average cost, posted to the inventory account, so the stock report always matches the balance sheet.
