Frequently asked questions

What is SmartTrader ERP?

A cloud app for Indian shops, traders and distributors that does GST billing, POS, khata, stock, purchase and double-entry accounts in one place, on mobile and computer.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan gives 50 GST invoices a month, 1 user, unlimited khata, POS and all reports, with no card needed. Paid plans start at Rs 999 a year.

Does it work in Hindi?

Messages to customers go in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and the help pages are in Hindi too.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Bills, receipts and expenses made offline are kept on the device and sent to the cloud when you are back online.

Is my data safe?

Each business is kept separate, passwords are hashed, staff get role-based access with an audit trail, and you can download a full backup or back up to your own Google Drive.

Can I move my data to Tally?

Yes. Export a Tally XML for any period; the trial balance matches.
