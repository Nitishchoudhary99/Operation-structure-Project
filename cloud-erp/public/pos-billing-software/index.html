Frequently asked questions

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on Android, iPhone and computers, and can be installed as an app.

Can I use the phone camera as a scanner?

Yes, on phones whose browser supports barcode detection; a USB or Bluetooth scanner works everywhere.

Can I sell on credit from the POS?

Yes. Choose Credit (udhaar); the amount goes to the customer’s khata.

What happens if the internet goes off?

Sales are kept in the Outbox on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns.
