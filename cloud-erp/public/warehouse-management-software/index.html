Receive by scan

Scan each piece against the supplier’s bill or order; anything not on it, or more than billed, is refused.

Racks chosen for you

Each item goes to its default bin, a bin that holds it already, or an empty one; new bins are made when needed.

Guided put-away

Frequently asked questions

Do I need special hardware?

No. A phone camera or any USB / Bluetooth scanner works; labels print on a normal or label printer.

Can it make bins automatically?

Yes. When goods are received and no bin fits, a new bin is made next to the last one (A-01 → A-02).

Does it support FEFO for batches?

Yes. Picking and billing take the batch that expires first.

Is there a gate entry?

Yes. The gate register logs vehicles and can read the e-invoice QR of supplier bills.
