Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. 50 invoices a month, 1 user, unlimited khata, POS and all reports, free forever.

Are prices per month or per year?

Per year. Starter is Rs 999, Growth Rs 2,499 and Business Rs 4,499 a year, plus 18% GST.

How do I upgrade?

Open Plan & Billing in the app and pay by UPI; the plan turns on as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Is my data safe?

Data is stored on Cloudflare, every business is kept separate, passwords are hashed, and staff get role-based access with an audit trail.
