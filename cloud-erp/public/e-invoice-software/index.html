From bill to portal

E-invoice JSON

The invoice is turned into the e-invoice schema (seller, buyer, items, HSN, tax) for upload on the IRP.

E-way bill JSON

Make the e-way bill JSON for the invoice, with vehicle and transport details, for the e-way bill portal.

IRN and EWB on the bill

Save the IRN, acknowledgement and e-way bill number back on the invoice; they print on the bill.

Supplier e-invoices at the gate

Scan the e-invoice QR on a supplier’s bill at the gate; the supplier and the bill are found by GSTIN and number.

GST summary

Frequently asked questions

Does it generate the IRN itself?

It prepares the e-invoice JSON in the official format; you upload it on the IRP and save the IRN on the bill. Direct generation through a GSP is not offered yet.

Can it make e-way bills?

It makes the e-way bill JSON for upload on the e-way bill portal and stores the e-way bill number on the invoice.

Which plan has e-invoice?

The Growth plan (Rs 2,499 a year) and the Business plan.

Does it read e-invoice QR codes of suppliers?

Yes. At the gate or on the Receive screen, scanning the QR fills in the supplier, bill number, value and IRN.
