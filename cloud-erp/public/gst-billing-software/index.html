Everything a GST bill needs

Correct GST, every time

Intra-state CGST + SGST or inter-state IGST is picked from the GSTIN / state. HSN codes, all GST slabs, round-off to the rupee.

Bill with UPI QR

Every invoice prints a UPI QR for the exact amount due, so customers pay on the spot with GPay, PhonePe or Paytm.

Share on WhatsApp

Send the bill as a link with a preview card, or as a real PDF from the phone. The customer can view it, download it and pay.

Quick bill / POS

Scan barcodes with a scanner or the phone camera, take cash, UPI or card, and print an 80 mm receipt.

MRP billing

Type prices with GST in them: Rs 10 MRP stays Rs 10 on the bill, and the GST is taken out of it to the paisa.

Returns & credit notes

Sales returns, purchase returns and rate-difference credit/debit notes that settle the original bill.

E-invoice & e-way bill

Download e-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill JSON in the government format and save the IRN back on the bill.

GST reports for your CA

  • GST summary by rate, B2B / B2C, output tax, input credit and net payable
  • Sales and purchase registers, exportable to CSV
  • Tally XML export with a matching trial balance
  • Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Trial Balance from real double-entry accounts

Bill designs and languages

  • Several invoice designs with a live preview; pick one per bill
  • Messages to customers in English, Hindi or Hinglish
  • Your logo, UPI ID, bank details and terms on every bill

Frequently asked questions

Is SmartTrader GST billing software free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 50 GST invoices a month, unlimited khata entries, POS billing and all reports. Paid plans start at Rs 999 a year.

Does it calculate CGST, SGST and IGST automatically?

Yes. It compares the state of your business and your customer (from the GSTIN or state name) and applies CGST + SGST or IGST on every line.

Can I send GST bills on WhatsApp?

Yes. Each bill has a WhatsApp button that sends a link with the amount, a preview card and a page where the customer can download the PDF and pay by UPI.

Does it support e-invoice and e-way bill?

It creates the e-invoice and e-way bill JSON files in the government format for upload on the portals, and stores the IRN and e-way bill number on the invoice.

Can my accountant use the data in Tally?

Yes. Export a Tally XML file for any period and import it into Tally Prime; the trial balance matches.
