Built for a busy counter

Scan and bill

Scan the barcode with a USB scanner or the phone camera; the item, rate and GST fill in. A carton code can count 12 pieces in one scan.

Cash, UPI, card or udhaar

Take any payment. For UPI the bill shows a QR for the exact amount; udhaar goes straight to the customer’s khata.

Udhaar khata customers trust

Every entry is sent to the customer on WhatsApp or SMS, and the customer confirms it on their own link.

Reminders without awkward calls

One tap sends the balance and your UPI. The reminder list says who to remind today and who is a VIP customer.

Low stock before it runs out

Set a reorder level per item; the Today card lists what is running low and makes one purchase order for all of it.

No internet? Keep billing

Bills made offline wait in the Outbox and go to the cloud by themselves when the internet returns.

A day at the shop

  1. Open the app: the Today card shows overdue customers, promises due today and low stock.
  2. Bill at the counter with the scanner; the customer pays by UPI from the QR.
  3. Note udhaar in the khata; the customer gets the entry on WhatsApp.
  4. In the evening, see the day’s sales, cash in hand and profit.

Reports a shop owner needs

  • Today’s sales, cash and UPI collected
  • Who owes how much, and since when
  • Profit & Loss and stock value
  • GST summary to send to your CA

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a barcode scanner?

Yes. Any USB or Bluetooth scanner works like a keyboard, and the phone camera can scan too. A code the app has not seen is taught once and works from then on.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Bills and receipts made offline are kept on the device and sent to the cloud automatically when you are back online.

Is the khata part of the billing app?

Yes. Udhaar from a bill goes to the same customer’s khata, so billing and khata always match.

Is it free for a small kirana shop?

The Free plan gives 50 GST invoices a month and unlimited khata. Unlimited bills start at Rs 999 a year.

Can I print a receipt?

Yes. Print an 80 mm thermal receipt or an A4 bill, or send the bill on WhatsApp.
