Har screen phone aur computer dono par chalti hai. Aapka data cloud me safe rehta hai, roz ka backup Cloudflare karta hai.

1. Shuru karein

  1. Browser me erp.docbrisk.com kholein aur Create a free account dabayein.
  2. Business ka naam, aapka naam, State (zaroori: isi se CGST+SGST ya IGST tay hota hai), GSTIN (agar hai), email aur password bharein.
  3. Login ke baad Settings → Business & Users kholein aur ye bharein: address, phone, UPI ID (jaise dukaan@okaxis), city, PIN code.
UPI ID zaroor bharein. Isse har bill aur khata message par Pay by UPI button aur QR aata hai, aur paisa jaldi milta hai.

2. App install karein

  • Android / Computer (Chrome): upar Install app button dabayein.
  • iPhone: Safari me Share → Add to Home Screen.

App icon se seedha khulta hai, aur Quick Bill, New invoice, Khata ke shortcuts milte hain.

Dashboard: is mahine ki sale, munafa, lena-dena, stock aur GST ek nazar me.

3. Items aur stock

  1. Items → + New item: naam, unit, HSN, GST %, purchase price, sale price, reorder level.
  2. Pehle se maal hai to Opening stock qty aur rate bharein.
  3. Barcode scan karke daal sakte hain; khali chhodne par app khud label code bana deta hai.
  4. Item page par Print barcode labels se stickers print karein.

Stock & Godowns me har item ka stock, average cost, value aur reorder status dikhta hai. Kam stock wale items ka PO ek click me: Raise a purchase order for low items (Advanced mode).

4. Customers aur suppliers

Customers & Suppliers → + New contact: naam, type, mobile (WhatsApp ke liye zaroori), GSTIN, state, credit days, credit limit. Purana baaki ho to contact khol kar Opening balance bharein.

5. GST invoice aur WhatsApp

  1. Invoices → + New (ya dashboard par + New invoice).
  2. Customer chunein, item chunein, qty likhein: rate aur GST apne aap aata hai.
  3. Paisa abhi mila to Received now me amount aur account (Cash / UPI / Bank) chunein.
  4. Save. Invoice number apne aap (INV-00001...).
  5. Invoice par WhatsApp dabayein: customer ko banner wala link jata hai jahan wo bill dekh, PDF download aur UPI se pay kar sakta hai.
  6. Print / PDF se A4 bill. Bill par amount in words aur UPI QR chhapta hai.
CGST+SGST ya IGST state se apne aap tay hota hai. Total rupaye me round hota hai.

6. Quick Bill (POS) aur barcode

  1. Menu me Quick Bill (POS).
  2. Barcode scanner se scan karein, ya Camera button se phone ka camera use karein, ya item ka naam likh kar Enter.
  3. Payment: Cash / UPI / Card / Credit (udhaar). UPI chunne par us amount ka QR screen par aata hai.
  4. Complete sale (F9): thermal receipt (80 mm) print hoti hai.

7. Purchase, returns, credit / debit note

  • Purchase Bills → + New: supplier ka bill. Stock badhta hai, supplier ka baaki banta hai.
  • Payments: supplier ko paisa diya to yahan entry.
  • Maal wapsi: invoice kholo → Return goods (Credit Note). Bill kholo → Return goods (Debit Note).
  • Rate difference / discount (maal nahi hilta): invoice kholo → Credit note, har line par per-unit difference likho. Supplier ke liye bill → Debit note.
  • Galat bill? Cancel dabayein. Stock aur hisaab dono apne aap ulte ho jaate hain. Kuch chhupke se delete nahi hota.

8. Khata Book: bharosemand udhaar

  1. Khata Book → + Add entry (naya contact) ya contact kholo → You gave ₹ / You got ₹.
  2. Amount aur note likho → Next: send to customer.
  3. Send on WhatsApp ya Send SMS dabao. Bhejte hi entry khate me judti hai.
Customer ko ek private link milta hai: wo apna poora khata dekhta hai, har entry par Yes / No dabata hai aur UPI se pay karta hai.
Bheji hui entry delete nahi hoti. Galti ho to Reverse dabao: ek correction entry banti hai jo customer ko bhi jati hai. Isi se dono taraf bharosa rehta hai.

9. Payment reminders

Payment Reminders screen par har wo customer dikhta hai jiska invoice ya khata baaki hai, sabse purane pehle. WhatsApp dabao: bills ki list, total aur UPI ke saath ready message jata hai. Bheje gaye reminder par sent ka nishaan lag jata hai.

11. Simple aur Advanced mode

Shuru me app Simple mode me hota hai: chhoti dukaan ke kaam ke menu hi dikhte hain. Settings → Features → Advanced chunne par ye bhi aa jaate hain:

  • Quotation, Sales Order, Delivery Note, Purchase Order, Goods Receipt (GRN)
  • Gate Entry (gaadi gate par aate hi entry, phir usse GRN) aur Gate Pass (maal bahar jaate waqt)
  • Stock Count (physical verification), Pick list (rack-wise), Pending Orders report
  • E-invoice aur e-way bill JSON (portal par upload ke liye)

Mode badalne se koi data delete nahi hota.

14. Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Internet chala jaye to?

App khulta hai, lekin nayi entry ke liye internet chahiye. Data cloud me safe rehta hai.

Galat entry kaise sudhaarein?

Bill: Cancel karke naya banayein. Khata: Reverse. Hisaab hamesha barabar rehta hai.

Customer ko Pay button nahi dikh raha?

Settings me apni UPI ID bharein.

Stock minus me kyun nahi jata?

Jitna maal hai usse zyada becha nahi ja sakta. Pehle purchase ya opening stock bharein.

CA ko data kaise dein?

Reports → GST Summary / Registers ka CSV, ya Tally Export ki XML file.

Madad chahiye?

erp.docbrisk.com par Plan & Billing page se WhatsApp karein.
